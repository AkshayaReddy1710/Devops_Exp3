Sridevi women's engineering was established in the year 2001 with the approval from AICTE, New Delhi and Government of A.P. The College is affiliated to J.N.T.U. Hyderabad and is sponsored by Sridevi Education Society. Our objective is to cultivate innovative women engineers through programs that are not only distinctive in their content but also exceptionally delivered to prepare them for real-world impact. Our total strength sums up to 3000. The EAMCET code is SDEW and college code is D2.

Sridevi Women’s Engineering College provides an excellent academic environment and latest learning resources.
